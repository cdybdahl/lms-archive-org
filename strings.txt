	EN	Rebuild Artist/Venue Index At

PLUGIN_ARCHIVELMA_REBUILD_HOUR_DESC
	EN	The local time of day to rebuild the Browse by Artist/Venue index in the background. A large collection like the whole Live Music Archive can take several minutes to fully index, so picking a quiet hour keeps it off any time you're likely to be listening. Also runs once shortly after the server starts, and again shortly after you change your collection list above.
