# String file for ArchiveLMA plugin

PLUGIN_ARCHIVELMA
	EN	Archive.org Browser

PLUGIN_ARCHIVELMA_DESC
	EN	Browse and stream one or more archive.org collections as a single merged catalog (defaults to the Aadam Jacobs Live Music Archive collection; configurable in Settings)

PLUGIN_ARCHIVELMA_SETTING_COLLECTIONS
	EN	Archive.org Collections

PLUGIN_ARCHIVELMA_SETTING_COLLECTIONS_DESC
	EN	The archive.org collections to browse, merged into one catalog. Find each identifier in its collection's URL: archive.org/details/&lt;identifier&gt;.

PLUGIN_ARCHIVELMA_COLLECTION_GAIN
	EN	Gain (dB)

PLUGIN_ARCHIVELMA_COLLECTION_GAIN_HINT
	EN	Volume adjustment in decibels for this collection. Positive boosts the volume, negative reduces it - whole numbers or decimals both work (e.g. 8, -3.5). Leave blank or 0 for no change. Only takes effect when "Compensate for Quiet Recordings" below is turned on.

PLUGIN_ARCHIVELMA_STREAMING_QUALITY
	EN	Streaming Quality

PLUGIN_ARCHIVELMA_STREAMING_QUALITY_DESC
	EN	Which format to prefer when archive.org has the same track in more than one - typically FLAC, a couple of MP3 encodes, and sometimes Ogg Vorbis. If your preferred format isn't available for a particular track, it automatically falls down the rest of the list in the order shown for whichever option you pick below.

PLUGIN_ARCHIVELMA_STREAMING_QUALITY_QUALITY
	EN	Highest Quality (FLAC > MP3 > Ogg Vorbis > low-bitrate MP3)

PLUGIN_ARCHIVELMA_STREAMING_QUALITY_BANDWIDTH
	EN	Lowest Bandwidth (MP3 > Ogg Vorbis > low-bitrate MP3 > FLAC)

PLUGIN_ARCHIVELMA_GAIN_ENABLED
	EN	Compensate for Quiet Recordings

PLUGIN_ARCHIVELMA_GAIN_ENABLED_DESC
	EN	Some archive.org collections (classical recordings especially) are mastered noticeably quieter than a typical local library - by as much as 10dB RMS. Turn this on to apply the per-collection dB boost set in the Gain column above during playback. This is independent of your player's own Replay Gain setting - it applies (or doesn't) based solely on this toggle.

PLUGIN_ARCHIVELMA_REBUILD_HOUR
	EN	Rebuild Artist/Venue Index At

PLUGIN_ARCHIVELMA_REBUILD_HOUR_DESC
	EN	The local time of day to rebuild the Browse by Artist/Venue index in the background. A large collection like the whole Live Music Archive can take several minutes to fully index, so picking a quiet hour keeps it off any time you're likely to be listening. Also runs shortly after you change your collection list above, or use Rebuild Index Now below. On a fresh install, or if "Rebuild Index on Server Restart" is on, it also runs once shortly after the server starts.

PLUGIN_ARCHIVELMA_REBUILD_ON_RESTART
	EN	Rebuild Index on Server Restart

PLUGIN_ARCHIVELMA_REBUILD_ON_RESTART_DESC
	EN	Off by default: the artist/venue index is saved to disk and reloaded on restart instead of being rebuilt from scratch, so Browse by Artist/Venue comes back instantly even on slower hardware like a Raspberry Pi. Turn this on to always rebuild it from archive.org on every restart instead.

PLUGIN_ARCHIVELMA_REBUILD_NOW
	EN	Rebuild Index Now

PLUGIN_ARCHIVELMA_REBUILD_NOW_DESC
	EN	Rebuild the Browse by Artist/Venue index immediately in the background, without waiting for the scheduled time above.

PLUGIN_ARCHIVELMA_REBUILD_NOW_TRIGGERED
	EN	Rebuild started in the background - this can take a few minutes for a large collection.
